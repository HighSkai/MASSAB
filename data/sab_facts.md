## Game overview

Steal a Brainrot is a Roblox game published by the Roblox group BRAZILIAN SPYDER. [source: https://games.roblox.com/v1/games?universeIds=7709344486]
Steal a Brainrot launched on Roblox in May 2025. [source: https://www.story-kitchen.com/news/story-kitchen-partnering-with-steal-a-brainrot-creators-on-feature-adaptation-of-roblox-game]
The official Roblox record for Steal a Brainrot shows the experience was created on May 16, 2025 at 00:49 UTC. [source: https://games.roblox.com/v1/games?universeIds=7709344486]
Steal a Brainrot servers hold a maximum of 8 players. [source: https://games.roblox.com/v1/games?universeIds=7709344486]
Roblox files Steal a Brainrot under the Simulation genre and the Tycoon subgenre. [source: https://games.roblox.com/v1/games?universeIds=7709344486]
The official description of Steal a Brainrot tells players to buy a Brainrot, steal Brainrots from other players, generate money, rebirth, and buy slaps and troll gear. [source: https://games.roblox.com/v1/games?universeIds=7709344486]
The official description of Steal a Brainrot credits Chris for letting the game use the Nyan Cat and WinnersTakesAll for the backpack system. [source: https://games.roblox.com/v1/games?universeIds=7709344486]
The Brainrots in Steal a Brainrot are characters based on the 2025 Italian brainrot memes. [source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot]
The gameplay loop of Steal a Brainrot has been compared to capture the flag. [source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot]
Steal a Brainrot gets a new update every Saturday, usually at 3 PM EST, and an Admin Abuse of about 30 to 60 minutes follows it. [source: https://stealabrainrot.fandom.com/wiki/Admin_Abuse]
The latest Steal a Brainrot update as of early November 2026 is Update 73 on October 31, 2026, with Update 74 listed for November 7, 2026. [source: https://stealabrainrot.fandom.com/wiki/Update_Log]
The Steal a Brainrot Fandom wiki counts 557 indexable Brainrots as of November 2026, or 571 with Lucky Blocks and other miscellaneous entries. [source: https://stealabrainrot.fandom.com/wiki/Brainrots]

## Bases, stealing and trading

The base in Steal a Brainrot stores your Brainrots, which generate cash over time, and it can have up to 3 floors. [source: https://stealabrainrot.fandom.com/wiki/Base]

The Lock Base in Steal a Brainrot starts at 60 seconds, gains 10 seconds per rebirth, and gains a fixed 10 seconds with the VIP gamepass. [source: https://stealabrainrot.fandom.com/wiki/Base]
The Red Carpet in Steal a Brainrot, also called the Conveyor Belt, is where Brainrots walk across the map to be bought. [source: https://stealabrainrot.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Carpet]
A Brainrot bought from the Steal a Brainrot Red Carpet can be bought by another player while it walks to the base for 150% of its base cost. [source: https://stealabrainrot.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Carpet]
When a Brainrot is being stolen in Steal a Brainrot, the base owner gets a message and an alarm sound. [source: https://stealabrainrot.fandom.com/wiki/Stealing]
A player carrying a stolen Brainrot in Steal a Brainrot cannot use gears, and their speed drops from 34 to 20 and their jump height from 7.2 to 4.32. [source: https://stealabrainrot.fandom.com/wiki/Stealing]
The Steal a Brainrot steal message uses the rarity color: green Common, blue Rare, purple Epic, yellow Legendary, red Mythic, rainbow Brainrot God, black and white Secret, black and yellow OG. [source: https://stealabrainrot.fandom.com/wiki/Stealing]
The Steal a Brainrot Index gives a +0.5x base multiplier for completing the Default, Gold, Diamond, or Rainbow Index, which each need 75% of the game's Brainrots. [source: https://stealabrainrot.fandom.com/wiki/Index]
The Trade Plaza in Steal a Brainrot is a place where stealing is disabled and has three sections: the normal plaza, the Pro plaza (25B/s), and the OG plaza (100B/s and an OG Brainrot). [source: https://stealabrainrot.fandom.com/wiki/Trading_Plaza]
Lucky Blocks in Steal a Brainrot can be bought and stolen like Brainrots, do not generate money, and open into a random Brainrot. [source: https://stealabrainrot.fandom.com/wiki/Lucky_Blocks]

Los Lucky Blocks in Steal a Brainrot are exclusive to Admin Abuse and cost $250,000,000 on the conveyor. [source: https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/steal-brainrot-rebirth-16-update-guide]
